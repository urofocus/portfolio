I look for weak spots, legally.

I practise ethical hacking on practice labs and challenges built for it. I write up each one in plain language so the next person can follow what I tried, what worked and how to fix it.

How I work

Rules I work by

Get permission first

I only test systems I own or have written permission to test. Practice labs and challenge platforms are where I learn.

Do no harm

I look for the weakness and stop there. I never take, change or delete data that is not mine.

Report clearly

Every finding gets a short write-up: what it is, how to see it, why it matters and how to fix it.
