About

Two jobs, one way of seeing.

I make visuals for people who need to be noticed, and I test systems for people who need them to hold up.

As a designer I take freelance orders for posters, logos, banners and product visuals. As an ethical hacker I practise on legal labs and practice platforms, and I write down what I learn so the next person can follow it.

Look closely

A weak margin or a weak password both hide in plain sight. I check the small things first.

Question the default

Why this font, why this port, why this permission. Defaults are where mistakes live.

Write it down

A design brief and a security report share one rule: if the reader cannot act on it, it is not finished.
